Hack in Hills '26 · Problem 03

One metal, four prices.

MCX lists four gold futures that differ only in size and purity. Converted to rupees per gram of pure gold, they should match. This lab measures where they don't, why, and whether the gap pays after real costs.

Term structure

Later expiries cost more

Holding gold for longer ties up money, so contracts that expire later trade higher. We measure that cost of carry from the data itself and use it to line GOLDM up with the others.

    Signals

    Each small contract is compared with its fair price: GOLDM moved to the same expiry, plus that contract's usual premium. A signal needs the gap to sit more than standard deviations from usual, using only past days.

    Why quiet is the right answer most days. In our sealed test, quiet-month signals lost money after costs. The gap paid only during sharp gold sell-offs, when GOLDM moved first and the small contracts lagged. A stress filter for that case is planned and will be tested on 2020–2023 data before it is trusted.
