    Hack in Hills ’26 · Problem 03

    One metal. Four prices.

    MCX lists four gold futures that differ only in size and purity: a 100 g bar, a 10 g bar, an 8 g coin and a 1 g piece. Per gram of pure gold, they should cost the same.

    Do they? And when they don’t, can anyone profit?

    Scroll

    Same unit · pure gold

    Strip away size and purity.

    Each price stands for a different amount of gold. Divide it by the pure gold inside, and all four are in one unit: rupees per gram of pure gold.

      Per gram of pure gold · 22 Oct 2026

      They nearly match.

      On 22 Oct 2026 the four closed within 65 bp of each other. Gaps like this open and close every day.

        Since 10 Oct 2023 · every trading day

        The gaps never sit still.

        How far each small contract sits from its usual premium over GOLDM, day by day. GOLDPETAL’s typical distance is 78 bp; on its wildest day it reached 801 bp.

        So a gap on its own means little. The question is whether it is big enough, and unusual enough, to act on.

        Check 1 · the cost line

        A gap has to pay for the trade.

        Buying one contract and selling another costs fees, taxes and slippage, in and out: 23.8 bp at 5 bp slippage a leg. Parity sets the bar at twice that, 47.6 bp.

          Check 2 · is it unusual?

          And it has to be rare for that contract.

          GOLDPETAL’s premium swings widely from day to day. Its 50.4 bp gap is only +0.33σ from its usual level. An alert needs ±1.5σ, which for GOLDPETAL means 227 bp.

          Both checks must pass.

          Alerts since 10 Oct 2023

          Most days, it stays quiet.

          66 alert episodes in 3 years across three contracts, about 1.8 a month. Each flare on the line is one.

          After an alert day the gap halved within 10 days 50% of the time, against 53% on ordinary days. An alert says “look”, not “trade”.

          How we know

          Every claim can be checked.

          • 23 yrs55,811 daily rows of official MCX Bhavcopy, 711 contracts since 20 Nov 2003
          • 3sealed tests: rules frozen in Git first, then run once on Aug 2025 – May 2026, 2016–2019 and 2020–2023
          • 10,408automated tests; the site rebuilds itself every trading day

          The honest result: no dependable edge after costs. The fair-price model made ₹42,834 over 2016–2023 at 5 bp, but its 95% range (−₹54,534 to ₹1,42,962) includes zero.
