# Intro models

All released under **CC0** (public domain, no attribution required; credited anyway).

## The props: [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com)

Downloaded at 1k and repacked with `gltf-transform optimize --compress meshopt --texture-compress webp --texture-size 512`
(the chest at 1024, keeping its lid and hasp as separate parts so the lid can open; the chest, lion head, chandelier and
first vase also simplified with `--simplify`).

## The gold: [TextureCan](https://www.texturecan.com) ("Free PBR Textures and CC0 3D Models")

Only the mesh and its normal map are kept; the colour comes from the intro's own gold materials.

| File | Source | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| gold_bar.glb | Gold Bar / Bullion / Ingot (Finance 0001), https://www.texturecan.com/models/details/530/ | stamped "FINE GOLD 999.9" normal map kept, 1024 px; simplified to 276 triangles |
